Use Mixtape in Codex

  1. Install the skill. 
    npx skills add m1nga/mixtape

  2. Open Codex or another assistant that supports skills. Paste an example or describe your own moment. Include where you listen if you have a preference.
  3. Review the tracklist. Open the Soundiiz link, connect your music account there, and confirm the import. Check the tracks in your music library.
