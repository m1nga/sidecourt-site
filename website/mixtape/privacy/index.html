Mixtape Privacy

CD Studio creates a preview in your browser. The title and ordered YouTube video IDs are encoded in the part of the link after #. Anyone with that link can open the mix. Keep personal information out of the title before sharing it.

The AI tool receives the title and ordered IDs you send to it, and returns a preview link. Mixtape does not save those inputs to a database. Your AI provider processes the conversation under its own privacy policy.

Playback loads an embedded YouTube player when you select Play mix. YouTube receives the video IDs and handles playback under its privacy policy. The public preview does not connect to your music account or access its library.
