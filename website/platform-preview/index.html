THE
INSIDE

What you keep hidden is what brought you here.

Most of what we show begins somewhere we don’t.

A feeling that something no longer fits.

An idea that refuses to leave.

The urge to step somewhere unfamiliar.

Something another person leaves with us.

We rarely arrive with a clean explanation.

Sometimes we make things simply because something inside us keeps moving.

If you’ve noticed the creatures around SideCourt, you’ve already seen traces of it.

A snake leaving what it has outgrown.

A tiger staying with something it cannot yet explain.

A leopard moving between land and water.

A magpie carrying something that did not begin with it.

They are not symbols to solve.

They are reminders that not everything that moves us needs to be visible.
Some things stay hidden for a while.

And sometimes,
that’s exactly what leads us here.

HOW IT WORKS

Simple on purpose.

Post what you made, ask for a hand, help someone back. From this site, or straight from the AI you already talk to.

01

Share a work

A title, a few lines, maybe a link or an image. It stays private until you say otherwise.

  1. Sign in and open Share something.
  2. Save a draft whenever you like. Nobody sees it yet.
  3. When it feels ready, submit. A person reads it, then you choose to publish.
Share a work →

02

Ask for a hand

No product needed. A question, a blocker, a next step you are unsure about.

  1. Open Ask for help and say what would help.
  2. People reply on your page. A reply never gives anyone editing access.
  3. Mark what helped. Close the request when you are done.
Ask for a hand →

03

Help someone

Browse DROPS, try something, reply with what you noticed. That already counts as showing up.

  1. Turn a card to read the request on its back.
  2. Reply, test, suggest, introduce.
  3. If it moved things forward, the author can say so.
See open requests →

04

Bring your AI

ChatGPT and Claude can work on your SideCourt draft, on your phone or your desktop. Claude Code and Codex too.

  1. In Your Court, open Your AI and copy one address.
  2. Paste it into your AI app as a connector. Sign in to SideCourt when it asks.
  3. Pick one draft and what the AI may do. Then just talk: “Save this idea to my SideCourt draft.”

Your AI never publishes, never sees your other drafts, and you can disconnect it any time. If ChatGPT says “Something went wrong” while you connect, that comes from ChatGPT: wait a minute and try again.

Connect your AI →

What stays yours

  • Private by default. Drafts, files and conversations with helpers stay private until you publish.
  • A person reads it first. Every post is reviewed by someone before it can go public. Files are checked before anyone can download them.
  • You hold the keys. Publishing, unpublishing, who may edit, and which AI may help are all your choices.
