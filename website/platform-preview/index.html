BEFORE YOU JOIN

A little structure. Room to be yourself.

What can I bring?

A work of your own with something people can inspect: a working link, a file, code, a guide, or other clear material. Finished and still-evolving works both fit. A request for help is optional.

How do I participate?

Season applications will open separately from publishing a Drop. For now, prepare your own work or explore another work. An invitation or a draft is not a confirmed Season entry.

What counts as useful help?

Say what you tried, what happened, and what you noticed. Respond to the author’s actual question. Do not assume that advice has been accepted or that a comment counts as a completed contribution.

Ownership, privacy and permissions

Your work remains yours. Do not include someone else’s private material or offer access you cannot grant. Sharing a work does not make it open source. No payment, prize or exchange of rights is part of this proposed gathering.

Accounts and work verification

Registration is open to anyone when account services are available. Sign in and set up your identity to leave public feedback; you do not need a reviewed work of your own. Coordinated collaboration proposals still require a reviewed work.

Dates, submission and what happens next

Opening and closing dates have not been set. No Season applications are being accepted yet. Feedback and collaboration do not depend on joining a Season.

THE INSIDE

What you keep hidden is what brought you here.

Most of what we show begins somewhere we don't.

A feeling that something no longer fits.
An idea that refuses to leave.
The urge to step somewhere unfamiliar.
Something another person leaves with us.

We rarely arrive with a clean explanation.

Sometimes we make things simply because something inside us keeps moving.

If you've noticed the creatures around SideCourt, you've already seen traces of it.

A snake leaving what it has outgrown.

A tiger staying with something it cannot yet explain.

A leopard moving between land and water.

A magpie carrying something that did not begin with it.

They are not symbols to solve.

They are reminders that not everything that moves us needs to be visible.

Some things stay hidden for a while.

And sometimes,

that's exactly what leads us here.

FROM THE AUTHOR

Why it exists.

Cleaning a keyboard should not mean accidentally setting things off. CleanPause makes room to wipe your keyboard, trackpad and screen.

Use & compatibility

Open CleanPause and start Cleaning Mode before wiping your device. Use the product website for current downloads, supported platforms and release details.
