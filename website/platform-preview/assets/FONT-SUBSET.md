# Inter web subset

InterLatin.woff2 is a Latin, Latin Extended and punctuation subset of the adjacent InterVariable.woff2, created with fontTools on 2026-09-16. The original optical-size and weight variation axes are retained. No glyph outlines were redesigned. The original full font remains the fallback for other scripts. Both files remain under the adjacent FONT-LICENSE.txt (SIL Open Font License 1.1).
