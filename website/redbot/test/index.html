Try RedBot in five minutes.

This test checks the new design and recommendation flow with original example notes. Xiaohongshu account sync is not available in this preview yet.

  1. Open RedBot. Before clicking, say what you think it can help you do.
  2. Choose Try with example notes, then pick or type a goal.
  3. Read the suggestion. Can you tell where it came from and what to do next?
  4. Open Short version & use. Can you distinguish saved notes from verified facts? Try the fictional free-tool example, edit your goal and copy its prompt.
  5. Choose Save for later. Refresh, find the saved plan and continue it.
  6. Try another suggestion or a goal with no matching source. Can you recover without help?

Do not ask the builder for hints until you are stuck. Mark anything you could not understand. Optional search by meaning downloads an AI model. The default preview does not require it.
