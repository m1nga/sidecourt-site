Try RedBot in five minutes.

No account connection. No installation. Please use an ordinary saved item, not private or sensitive material, for this first test.

  1. Open RedBot and try an example goal.
  2. Add one of your own links. Add a title and a short useful passage or note.
  3. Describe what you need in different words. Can you find the relevant material?
  4. Optionally enable AI on Wi-Fi, then repeat. If it fails, tell us and use basic matching.
  5. Keep a plan, refresh and find it again. Export a backup.

Do not ask the builder for hints until you are stuck. Mark anything you could not understand. The first AI download can be slow; this is part of the test.
