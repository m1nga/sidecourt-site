The public webpage does not connect to Xiaohongshu yet. It works with examples and material already in your browser. The existing Mac connector can read a signed-in account, but its scans are partial. Neither is a complete account-sync service.

A few things to know.

Where do my saves live?

In this browser on this device. My data and settings lets you export a backup or restore one. There is no automatic cloud or phone-to-computer sync. Existing saves stay intact when this preview updates. Do not share private backups in public feedback.

What does AI search do?

Optional search by meaning can find saved text even when you use different words. It runs on your device after downloading a model, roughly120 MB plus runtime, from Hugging Face and jsDelivr. These providers receive normal network request information. Basic matching works without the download.

It compares up to300 saves and methods. It can miss or misrank results. It does not watch a video or fetch the contents of a private post from its title. Read the source before relying on a suggestion.

Can I use RedBot in my AI assistant?

Yes. The companion skill reads a backup you choose, pasted notes, or an explicitly selected running local Mac library. Its assistant provider and privacy settings are separate from this browser preview. See the skill and installation steps →

How can I help test it?

Try a goal, read the suggested evidence, and see whether the next step is useful. Leave structured feedback. This preview has builder-operated checks; independent user testing is still needed.
