FROM THE AUTHOR

Why it exists.

Work piles up as text you never get to read twice. EarBrief turns the pieces worth keeping into something worth hearing, so a walk or a kitchen counter becomes a place to think.

How it works ＋

Sign in with your email. Paste one API key from a free AI service (Google Gemini worldwide, SiliconFlow in mainland China). Then paste text or upload a PDF, choose Chinese or English, and lock your phone. The episode is produced in the cloud and appears in your station.

Runs in any modern browser on iPhone, Android, Mac and Windows; no app store needed. Your AI key is stored encrypted and used only for your episodes. Free tiers have daily limits, so long episodes are made in parts.

SIDECOURT NOTE
