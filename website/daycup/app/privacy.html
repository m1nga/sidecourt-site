PRIVACY & YOUR DATA

Your coffee. Your choice.

DayCup calculates recipes on your device. Guest records stay in this browser or iPhone library. Signing in alone does not upload them. After you explicitly open or copy to your private cloud library, changes sync to that account. There is no tracking SDK or subscription requirement.

Photo reading is optional.

On iPhone, Apple Vision reads label text on the device. It does not send the photo to OpenAI. Review the text: a photo cannot confirm basket capacity, grinder calibration or how a coffee tastes.

On a web edition with photo reading connected, you can activate it with a DayCup invitation. Each time you choose AI photo reading, DayCup asks before sending the selected, resized image through its server to OpenAI. Your coffee history is not sent. The server keeps a hashed session identifier, an invitation-linked user ID and request dates, status and token counts to enforce usage limits. It does not save photo bytes or recognized label text. OpenAI processes submitted images under its API data policy. You can always enter the label instead. The standalone HTML edition cannot contact the photo service.

Your cup image.

Each saved cup starts with DayCup branding, your taste expression and three measured numbers. You can add details, change the displayed wording or use your own photo. These edits affect only the image, not the measured cup. Photos are resized and re-encoded on this device without location metadata. They sync with the private library only when account sync is active. Share drafts and their photos are included in library backups and require DayCup 2.8 or newer. Private feedback notes are excluded from the image. Saving an image does not publish it. Choose a destination yourself.

Built-in scenes are AI-created artwork, not real-location photos. Scene choices are saved with your design and work offline. Designs with scene backgrounds require DayCup 2.9 or newer. When sharing to Circle, you preview the finished image and can choose whether to include it.

City connections.

You can choose an available city to see approximate city-to-city distances. DayCup does not request GPS or collect a home address. The preference stays on this device and is not part of library backups. Including the city with a public cup is a separate unchecked choice each time. You can clear the preference from Circle or by clearing the local library; removing a public cup withdraws its city too. Public responses contain a city label and rounded distance, not device coordinates.

Exporting a cup file.

Exporting a shared cup includes the name you enter, that bean and batch, equipment names, actual measurements, taste and the bag reflection for that drink. It excludes photos, private notes and your other cups. Review the preview before sharing. Nothing is automatically posted to SideCourt. Imported shared cups remain separate from your own taste history; the author name is supplied by the sender, not independently verified.

Back up what matters.

A downloaded HTML file contains the app, not your later records. Export a JSON backup from My data before changing browser or device, or clearing storage. Backups include photos, preferences, private notes and reflection revisions. Taste Match backups require DayCup 2.4 or newer; older DayCup backups remain supported.

Replacing a shelf photo removes its older image from the current shelf library. Candidate edits and reflections retain earlier records in backups; removing a contender only removes it from the active shortlist. Previously exported files keep their original contents. Clear this local library removes all its records. Received cup files can also be removed individually.

Local records and exported files are not encrypted by DayCup. Anyone using this browser profile can access them. A SideCourt subpath is not a separate security origin; trusted scripts on the same origin can access its storage. iPhone system backups follow your device settings.

Old local libraries remain separate until you explicitly recover one. Current private account libraries are excluded from guest recovery. Clearing local data does not delete files you already shared or exported. Persistent storage is best effort; private browsing is not a reliable long-term library. The web host may keep access logs under its own policy.

TheClub

Your library is private by default. Sign in with your DayCup / SideCourt account and choose a public name to join TheClub. Only the saved cups you explicitly publish, their finished share images and captions become public. Private notes and other history are excluded. You can cheer, follow, hide a person, report a concern, remove your own shared cups or leave TheClub. Reports are stored for review. Copies saved by others cannot be recalled. TheClub does not use OpenAI tokens.

TheClub profiles and interactions use Supabase. Account credentials are kept in iPhone Keychain or browser local storage so you can stay signed in; community credentials use Keychain or tab session storage. Neither is included in library backups. Account cloud data and public posts are stored separately. Public text has a limited automatic check, not comprehensive moderation. For a report, safety or removal request, contact Community support.

Optional account sync

Use Google or email to sign in or register with the shared SideCourt account service. Choose to open a private cloud library, start an empty one or bring your guest records. Your activated library reopens on this device and syncs changes; guest data is never uploaded automatically. From My account you can sign out, delete the cloud copy or delete your DayCup account data and TheClub profile. Deleting DayCup data keeps the shared SideCourt sign-in and other products intact. A content-free revision marker prevents an older device from restoring erased data. Export a backup before deletion.
