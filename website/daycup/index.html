Keep it on your home screen

  1. iPhone / iPad: open DayCup in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.
  2. Android: open it in Chrome, tap the menu, then Install app or Add to Home screen.
  3. Desktop: Use the Install option in Chrome or Edge if it is available.

Installation options depend on your browser. You can also download a separate offline copy below.

Where your records live

Setups, beans and brew records stay in this browser. They are not automatically copied to another device. Export a backup from My data before changing browser or device, then import it where you want to continue.
