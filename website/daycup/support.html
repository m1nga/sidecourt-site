A little help with your next cup.

For Daycup questions or a problem you cannot resolve, email getem@sidecourt.space. Include your Daycup version, iPhone/iOS or browser version, what you tried and what happened. Please omit private records and photos unless requested.

Get started

Add your brewer and grinder under Setup, confirming your basket and usable dose. Add a bag under Beans. Open Brew for an espresso, Americano, iced Americano or V60 starting recipe. Record what you actually used and tasted in Journal.

Back up or move your library

Open Settings and backup on iPhone, or My data on the web. Export a JSON backup and save it somewhere you trust. Import that file on your other device. Daycup does not automatically sync records between devices or between its app and web editions.

Missing records

Check that you opened the same app or browser profile. Browser private mode, a different browser, clearing site data or uninstalling the app can make a library unavailable. Restore an exported backup if you have one. Do not clear your library while investigating.

Using it offline

The iPhone app includes its interface and recipe library. For the web edition, first open it online and allow the offline files to finish loading. Download an offline copy from the Daycup page if your browser does not support installation.

Recipe and grinder guidance

Recipes are starting points. Confirm the actual capacity and calibration of your equipment, and follow the manufacturer's instructions. The dial records a setting; it does not control your grinder. Taste and measured results guide adjustments.
