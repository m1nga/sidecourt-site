A little help with your next cup

Your account

Choose Sign in / Register, then Google or email. Email verification also creates a new account. DayCup and SideCourt share your sign-in identity. Open your private cloud library to sync equipment, beans, preferences and cups. Guest records remain separate until you choose Bring my guest records.

Your first cup

Open You → Your setup. Choose a brewer and grinder from the catalog, or enter their names manually. Save the models first; confirm the actual basket or dripper capacity when making your first recipe. Add a bag under Beans, choose a drink under Brew, then record how your cup tasted.

Edit a bag or choose Delete bag on its card. Deleting a bag preserves saved cup snapshots, recipes and share images in your history.

Share with TheClub

Every saved cup opens its editable share image. Use your photo or a built-in scene, move the logo and parameters, then save the image or share it to TheClub. Joining asks for a public name; it never publishes your private library automatically. Cheers, follows and Brew this connect one cup to another.

Backup and account removal

Settings offers an exported backup. My account offers cloud save, refresh, sign-out and deletion. Delete my DayCup account removes DayCup cloud content and TheClub membership; it keeps the shared SideCourt sign-in and other products intact. Saved copies on other devices cannot be remotely erased.

Get in touch

Email getem@sidecourt.space for account help, community reports or removal requests. Include your DayCup version and what happened. Omit private photos and records unless requested. A shared cup’s menu also lets you report a concern or hide that person.

为你的下一杯，提供一点帮助

登录与注册

选择「登录 / 注册」，使用 Google 或邮箱验证，新用户验证后即可创建账号，DayCup 与 SideCourt 共用登录身份，打开私人云端库后同步器材、豆子、偏好和冲煮记录，本机访客记录保持独立，只有你选择「带上本机访客记录」才会上传

开始第一杯

进入「我的 → 你的器材」，从目录选择咖啡机和磨豆机，也可以手动填写，先保存型号，生成第一杯配方时再确认实际粉碗或滤杯容量，在「豆子」添加一包豆子，到「冲煮」选择饮品，最后记下这杯的口感

豆子卡片可以编辑或删除，删除豆子会保留历史里的单杯记录、配方快照和分享图

分享到 TheClub

保存一杯后即可编辑分享图，使用自己的照片或内置背景，调整标识与参数的位置，再保存图片或分享到 TheClub，加入时设置公开昵称，私人记录不会自动公开，可以致意、关注，或跟做一杯并分享自己的结果

备份与删除账号

设置里可以导出备份，「我的账号」提供云端保存、刷新、退出和删除，删除 DayCup 账号会移除它的云端内容与 TheClub 资料，保留共用的 SideCourt 登录身份及其他产品，已经保存在其他设备上的副本无法远程删除

联系我们

账号问题、社区举报或移除请求，请联系 getem@sidecourt.space，说明 DayCup 版本和遇到的问题，请勿主动发送私人照片或完整记录，共享单杯的菜单也可以举报或隐藏用户
