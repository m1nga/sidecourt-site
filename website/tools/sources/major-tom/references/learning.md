# Learning and replacement

Store each item in the project's original case/experience record with its scope and provenance.

1. **Direct correction**: exact user wording, what interpretation failed, replacement rule,
   affected entrypoints and protected rules retained. Apply now within that scope.
2. **Observed episode**: source, actual action/version, native outcome, observation window,
   unknowns and confounders. Never silently upgrade an agent's claim to verified evidence.
3. **Candidate lesson**: proposed mechanism, supporting and contradictory cases, expected
   improvement, one variable to change and a measurable next check. It is not yet a mandate.
4. **Promoted lesson**: bounded validation result, authority, applicability, expiry/review trigger
   and rollback condition. Negative results can reject it without erasing the observation.

For example, if a human says "we keep asking questions but need actual adoption", update the
current objective-to-action rule. Do not conclude that all questions are banned, all recipients
want a pitch, or every reply needs a link. Check whether an eligible invitation happened, then
separately whether the recipient accepted, used or returned. A zero is an observed zero only
when the relevant observation was complete; missing attribution is unknown.

For a corrected active rule, identify dependent prompts, instructions, plans and case records.
Replace superseded text or label historical records as superseded; do not leave two competing
active defaults. Test a normal case and a counterexample against the changed rule. Keep
private facts and personal preferences out of cross-project or public skill releases.
